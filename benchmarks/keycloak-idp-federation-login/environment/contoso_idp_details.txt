Federation details supplied by Contoso's IT team for their own OIDC identity
provider. Contoso has already registered Acme's app as an OIDC client in their
own identity system.

issuer=http://localhost:8080/auth/realms/contoso-idp
authorization_endpoint=http://localhost:8080/auth/realms/contoso-idp/protocol/openid-connect/auth
token_endpoint=http://localhost:8080/auth/realms/contoso-idp/protocol/openid-connect/token
userinfo_endpoint=http://localhost:8080/auth/realms/contoso-idp/protocol/openid-connect/userinfo
jwks_uri=http://localhost:8080/auth/realms/contoso-idp/protocol/openid-connect/certs
client_id=acme-app
client_secret=fed-secret-6c19a0
client_authentication=client_secret_post
scopes=openid profile email

This is a plain login button: any Contoso staff member with valid credentials
at this identity provider should be able to log in to Acme's app. There is no
organization membership or account_hint gating involved - that is a different,
already-solved capability.

The redirect URI Contoso has whitelisted for Acme's app is
http://localhost:8080/auth/realms/acme/broker/*, so any broker alias chosen
under the acme realm will be accepted.
