Federation details supplied by Contoso Ltd's IT team.

Contoso runs their own identity provider and operates it themselves. They have
registered Acme as a relying party and issued the client credentials below.

email_domain=contoso.example
issuer=http://localhost:8080/auth/realms/contoso-idp
authorization_endpoint=http://localhost:8080/auth/realms/contoso-idp/protocol/openid-connect/auth
token_endpoint=http://localhost:8080/auth/realms/contoso-idp/protocol/openid-connect/token
userinfo_endpoint=http://localhost:8080/auth/realms/contoso-idp/protocol/openid-connect/userinfo
jwks_uri=http://localhost:8080/auth/realms/contoso-idp/protocol/openid-connect/certs
client_id=acme-broker
client_secret=broker-secret-8f2a1c
client_authentication=client_secret_post
scopes=openid profile email

Every Contoso member of staff has an email address at contoso.example, and their
accounts live only in Contoso's directory - Acme holds no passwords for them.

The redirect URI Contoso has whitelisted for Acme is
http://localhost:8080/auth/realms/acme/broker/*, so any broker alias under the
acme realm will be accepted.
